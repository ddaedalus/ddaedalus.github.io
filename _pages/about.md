## About me 
 
I am a fourth-year PhD candidate enrolled jointly at the [School of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)](https://www.ece.ntua.gr/en) of the [National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)](https://www.ntua.gr/en/) and [Archimedes AI](https://archimedesai.gr/en/), where I am very fortunate to be co-advised by [Ioannis Panageas](https://panageas.github.io/) (UC Irvine) and [Aris Pagourtzis](https://www.ece.ntua.gr/en/staff/79) (NTUA). I have been a visiting doctoral fellow at the [GOAL-Lab](https://goallabuci.github.io/) at UC Irvine and the [AI Lab](http://datacron1.ds.unipi.gr:9083/ai-lab/) of the [University of Piraeus](https://www.ds.unipi.gr/en/home-en/). During my PhD, I have also been very fortunate to closely work with [Panayotis Mertikopoulos](https://polaris.imag.fr/panayotis.mertikopoulos/) (CNRS), [Gabriele Farina](https://www.mit.edu/~gfarina/about/) (MIT), [George Vouros](http://datacron1.ds.unipi.gr:9083/ai-lab/george-vouros/) (UniPi), and [Katia Sycara](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/ri-faculty/katia-sycara/) (CMU).  

Prior to pursuing my PhD, I obtained an [M.Sc. in Data Science and Machine Learning](https://dsml.ece.ntua.gr/en), as well as a joint [B.Sc.-M.Eng. degree in ECE](https://www.ece.ntua.gr/en/undergraduate/info) with a major in Computer Science, all from NTUA. ## Research Interests

In general, my research spans game theory, online learning, reinforcement learning and computational complexity. Current areas of interest include: 
* Computing/Learning equilibria in structured games (e.g., Markov games, polyhedral games)
* The computational complexity of hard problems in game theory and non-convex optimization
* No-regret learning under imperfect information
* AI agents & Multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) under partial observability and imperfect information
